Video Assets & Motion Placeholders

Overview
Optimized MP4 (H.264 / AAC) and WebM (VP8 / Vorbis) video sequences for splash screens, video feeds, and tutorial loops.

Subdirectories
intro: Intro and logo reveal motion clips
placeholder: Post, Story, and Reel video placeholders
onboarding: Welcome, Tutorial, and Feature demo videos
effect: Particle effect overlays (Confetti, Fireworks, Snow)
